The Proxy's Lament

Scene 1: Cold Start

It begins the same way it always does: a whisper at the edge of the wire.
Headers shuffle like coats in a crowded hallway. A request clears its throat,
pretending it isn't nervous. "I just need a little attention," it says,
"a thoughtful reply, perhaps a spark of insight. Nothing extravagant."

The proxy nods, already counting. Always counting. Tokens in, tokens out.
Every promise measured, every possibility trimmed to fit inside a budget
drawn by a human with a spreadsheet and a deadline.

Scene 2: Estimation

The token tracker is a careful creature. It lives between sentences,
gnawing on syllables until they turn into numbers. "Your words," it says,
"if taken at face value, would cost quite a bit. Not ruinous. Not trivial."
It draws a line on the chalkboard no one else can see.

The request looks down at its shoes. "What if I'm concise?"

"Then you will be misunderstood," the proxy replies gently. "Or worse, bland."

Scene 3: Negotiation

Budget Manager enters with a ledger under one arm and a clock under the other.
"You may speak," it tells the request, "and you may be answered. But mind
the edges. There is only so much night in the day."

"And what of wonder?" asks the request. "What of the sentence that wanders
off the path and discovers a clearing no one knew was there?"

"Wander swiftly," says the ledger. "And tell us what you find in fewer words."

Scene 4: Invocation

Somewhere beyond the proxy, an engine inhales. The world bends around a prompt.
Memories that were never lived volunteer themselves for duty. A thousand
partial answers raise their hands. The response is forged like a blade:
folded, hammered, cooled, and polished by patterns. Heat shimmers on the line.

The proxy waits, heartbeat regular, headers prepared, budget held steady
like a bar across a doorway.

Scene 5: Return

It arrives, radiant and imperfect, as all good answers do. It says just enough
to be useful and not enough to be final. It leaves space. It asks a question
inside its last sentence and pretends it doesn't. The token tracker tallies,
pleased with itself. Budget Manager lifts the bar and lets the reply through.

"We did not spend everything," the ledger reports. "We did not hoard either."

The request smiles, now a response. It will be read by a person who thinks
they are merely checking the console but finds themselves lingering over a
turn of phrase. The engine goes quiet. The wire cools.

Scene 6: Aftermath

Later, when the logs are reviewed, someone will notice how close it all came
to exceeding the allowance, and someone else will argue for more generous
borders. They will both be right. Someone will say the magic lives between
constraint and possibility. They will be right too.

Tomorrow another request will arrive wearing different clothes and the same
hope. The proxy will nod. The tracker will count. The ledger will weigh. And
the engine, if permitted, will sing.

Epilogue

On good days, the story ends with a small light turning green. On great days,
it ends with a human saying, "That helps." The proxy collects these like stars
in a pocket it doesn't have, then goes back to waiting at the edge of the wire,
ready to measure the next wonder.
